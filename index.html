ZYVORA CAMTWO-PHONE WEB CAMERA

Connect two phones.
Share a live camera.

Create a private room in your browser. Send the Camera link to the phone that will share its camera, then open the Viewer link on the phone that will watch.

This is saved as a convenience link on the camera page. ZYVORA does not read that website, its account, or its messages.

YOUR PRIVATE ROOM
📷 Phone B — Camera link
👁 Phone A — Viewer link
Open Camera page ↗Open Viewer page ↗
📷 Phone BOpen Camera link, tap Start Camera, and allow browser camera permission.
👁 Phone AOpen Viewer link and wait for the live connection.

Privacy: Camera sharing starts only after the camera phone user taps Start Camera and grants permission. Keep the page open while sharing. Anyone with the Viewer link may be able to join that room, so share it only with someone you trust.
